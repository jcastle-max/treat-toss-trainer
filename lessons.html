ObjectiveStudents will identify the parts of a lever (fulcrum, load, effort) and explain how a catapult launches an object.

A seesaw is a lever. Where is the fulcrum?

Show the lever parts

In a treat toss, the candy is the load, the pivot is the fulcrum, and the push or rubber band is the effort.

Meet your client: Mr. Jones

Mr. Jones has the flu. He doesn't want to spread his germs to trick-or-treaters, so he needs a way to launch candy from his door to the kids on the sidewalk. He's also feeling weak, so the device can't take much strength to use.

Sketch one design each

  • Arm: the stick that swings
  • Candy holder: what keeps the candy in place
  • Power source: what makes it launch (rubber band, clothespin spring, a push)
